# Running performance analytics

This update extends the existing Mac companion. Garmin access, credential handling, FIT ingestion, local SQLite records, loopback server and four Finder launchers retain their existing architecture. Garmin requests still use only the official client. No hosted runtime or external analysis service is involved.

## Views and interaction

- **Run Comparison:** select 2–5 runs in history, in the comparison picker or from Run Detail. Similar-run pairs can be added together. Compare summary metrics, half-run efficiency, drift, matched effort, splits and late-run changes. Pace, HR, cadence and elevation overlays support percentage of elapsed activity or distance; legends retain each selected run's colour. HR/pace scatter and per-kilometre HR/pace are included.
- **Aerobic Efficiency / HR at Pace:** selectable 5 bpm bands (100–200) and 15 sec/km pace bands (3:00–10:15/km), duration filters, global dates/distance/classification, source-run links, matched minutes, sample strength, recent best, 28-day rolling means and 4/12-week changes. Overview chooses the aerobic band with most matched minutes among 140–165 bpm.
- **Fitness Trends:** separate aerobic pace, HR at pace, decoupling, 5K performance, longer-run speed/HR and cadence stability. Short/medium/long changes compare adjacent 4-week/12-week/6-month windows. No composite fitness score or dependence on VO2 max.
- **5K Performance:** sensor-validated best segments, imported PB progression and event history, recent best, rolling trend, average/max HR, cadence, split CV, half change and final kilometre. Activity distance identifies near-5K recordings; race/time-trial/parkrun title cues remain explicitly provisional. Incidental segments are distinguished. One primary table combines sensor-validated and credible Garmin-reported results, with evidence badges and expandable validation diagnostics. Both credible tiers enter PB/record progression, Overview, fitness trends and insights; flagged results remain visible but excluded. Unavailable segment-specific sensor metrics remain null. A near-5K activity without an exact reported or reconstructed split is not promoted to an exact 5K result.
- **Personal Records:** 400 m (only with fine sampling), 1 km, 1 mile, 3 km, 5 km, 10 km, longest summary-distance run, average pace at each segment distance, previous-record improvement and progression history. Tiles, points and event rows open the source run. These are bests within imported history, not certified race or lifetime records.
- **Training Load:** weekly/monthly distance, rolling 7/28-day distance, counts, average distance, longest run, long-run share, weekly changes, prior four-week baseline and intensity-weighted minutes. Calendar weeks start Monday. Current incomplete weeks are labelled.
- **Recovery:** selectable normalised timelines for HRV, resting HR, sleep, sleep score, Body Battery, stress, previous-day distance and rolling volume. Explore each recovery metric against fixed-pace HR or volume with same-day, previous-day or exact two-day lag. Raw observations and source-run links remain visible; additional individual wellness charts can be expanded.
- **Calendar:** historical month/week navigation, day classification, distance, pace/HR (desktop), weekly totals and activity links. Classification colour is restrained.
- **Observations:** ranked deterministic local rules for meaningful aerobic changes, PB improvements, abrupt volume, sustained RHR deviation, decoupling changes, progressively faster comparable runs and short-sleep associations. No observations are emitted to fill space. Each includes its sample and limitations, and links to a supporting view.
- **Overview:** current training, measured fitness changes and drift, recent 5K best, recovery, high-value observations and classified recent activity. Mobile run tables become readable activity cards; wide comparison/evidence tables scroll inside bounded regions.

Global filters stay while navigating. Period, classification, band and comparison selection use session storage. Date/distance/class filters apply to analysis views. Record PBs deliberately retain all imported history; calendar and manual comparison deliberately browse all history. Observation windows are anchored to the selected end date and can use earlier history for baselines, irrespective of the chart start date. The Settings help documents the methods.

## Quality and methods

**Shared gates.** Accept moving intervals with positive time steps ≤10 s, speed 0.5–10 m/s and consistent cumulative distance. Reject HR outside 35–220 bpm or changing >5 bpm/s; missing/invalid HR stays null. Cadence outside 80–240 steps/min stays null. Gaps and pauses reduce coverage rather than disappearing from the elapsed clock. The gates identify obvious errors, not every optical cadence lock or GPS problem. Sensor streams covering under 95% of the activity summary distance are explicitly flagged as incomplete.

**Comparable effort.** Remove ten minutes of warm-up. Retain only paired speed/HR bouts ≥30 continuous seconds inside the selected half-open band, with ≥180 matched seconds per run. Pace is 1000 divided by time-weighted mean speed; inverse analysis uses time-weighted mean HR. Each rolling point averages qualifying run observations within 28 calendar days. Changes need at least three runs in both adjacent windows; recent best needs three recent runs. These are unadjusted comparisons, not weather/grade corrections or statistical confidence intervals.

**Decoupling.** Remove ten minutes; require at least twenty further minutes. Split remaining elapsed time into equal halves; require ≥80% reliable paired coverage per half and speed CV ≤15%. Efficiency is time-weighted mean speed / time-weighted mean HR. Decoupling is `100 × (first efficiency − second efficiency) / first efficiency`. Positive means less speed per heartbeat later. Matched-band half changes use common 5 bpm / 15 sec/km bands with ≥120 s in each half, then average shared-band differences. This is a steady-run descriptor, not a medical or heat-adjusted assessment.

**Segments.** Search recorded sample starts with distance-crossing interpolation at the end, using elapsed time. For 5K only, permit up to three isolated questionable episodes: at most 12 seconds total AND 1% of candidate elapsed time, at most 8 consecutive seconds and at most 30 m of affected travelled distance. Small speed/distance disagreement is tolerable only up to 20 m per interval. Elapsed slowdown time is retained. Reject sustained stationary sections, gaps >10 s, cumulative resets, implied distance speed >10 m/s and overall 5K speed >7 m/s. Other record distances retain strict interval validation. See FIVE_K.md for the evidence hierarchy and diagnostic details. Omit 400 m unless the recording has ≤2 s sample gaps. End-to-end descent >1% flags the performance and excludes it from PBs; missing elevation is labelled unverified. Sampling precision is disclosed. No distance-based algorithm can certify GPS accuracy. Partial streams can provide a segment only if that segment itself is complete and reliable. Longest run uses the Garmin activity summary distance, explicitly labelled separately.

**Classification and matching.** Classification combines Garmin HR-zone distribution, duration, sustained 30-second pace-block changes and title cues. Threshold/VO2 names are heuristic labels rather than measured physiological thresholds. Ambiguity remains unclassified. Local overrides can be reset to automatic. Similar runs require distance within 25% and duration within 35%; rank distance and duration plus available HR, pace, ascent and category. Better/similar/worse uses the observed speed/HR ratio with ±2% descriptive tolerance. No exact route matching or environmental adjustment is claimed.

**Recovery.** Standardised overlays show `(value − filtered mean) / filtered standard deviation`; raw units remain in the evidence table. Running performance is HR at 5:30–5:45/km using the same quality gates. Average multiple qualifying runs on a day to one observation. Pearson association needs ten paired days and nonzero variance. Lags use the exact previous date, without carrying missing values forward. Repeated observations and confounding limit interpretation; association never implies causation.

**Load and observations.** Rolling sums include the selected day and zero-run days. Zone-weighted minutes sum zone minutes × zone index 1–5; they are only a descriptive intensity/load proxy. An abrupt volume flag needs >30% and ≥5 km over the preceding four calendar weeks. It is not an injury threshold or the “10% rule.” Aerobic insights need ≥3 runs per four-week window and ≥5 sec/km difference; drift insights need ≥3 per window and ≥1.5 percentage points; short-sleep associations need ≥5 paired days in each group and ≥3 bpm; RHR observations need ≥21 baseline days and three consecutive days ≥5 bpm above baseline. PB insights need at least two recent PB events and ≥5 seconds improvement.

## Storage, upgrades and performance

SQLite migration version 2 adds `derived_runs(id, fingerprint, payload)`, `run_overrides(id, classification)` and `records_dataset_fetched`. Existing records/metadata, database path and credentials are retained. No records are deleted or reset. Derived results invalidate by source fetched timestamps and method version; changed runs alone need sensor recomputation. Summary queries read cached derived JSON without loading years of streams. A selected run's chart response is explicitly projected and bounded to roughly 2,400 display samples; calculations use full local recordings.

There are **no new production Python or JavaScript dependencies**. Playwright is a development-only QA tool, outside Mac requirements. Compiled React assets are committed. Node/npm remain unnecessary on the Mac.

Close the running dashboard, pull/merge the full update (including `companion/static`), then open **Open Garmin Dashboard.command**. The additive migration and first derived-cache build happen automatically; that first opening can take longer on a large history. Run **Refresh Garmin Data.command** normally. **Setup does not need rerunning**, and no key re-entry or database deletion is needed. Full History remains optional for revisiting older edits or missing originals.

Validate original FIT availability, real sensor units/quality, classification against your configured zones, elevation flags, summary stride/elapsed fields and wellness availability after your normal local refresh. Unknown or absent optional Garmin fields remain unavailable. macOS Finder/Gatekeeper execution and account-specific Garmin retention cannot be exercised in Linux synthetic QA.

## 5K evidence and FIT revalidation update

Analysis version 7 automatically recomputes existing cached activities without deleting records or changing SQLite schema version 2. Existing `fastestSplit_5000` values participate immediately after reopening, without a Garmin refresh. Routine Refresh retries missing, failed and incomplete series with bounded cooldown and tries reparsing a local original first. Changed source summaries trigger a fresh original download. Use **Revalidate activity data** in Run Detail to force a single official FIT download/reparse immediately; it bypasses cooldown. Refresh Full History intentionally revisits all originals and is optional, not needed for this PB correction. No Setup rerun or new dependency is required.
